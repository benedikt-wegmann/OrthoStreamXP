## What's new

- **Dynamic quality.** A new preset in **Settings** (and in the setup): **Dynamic**. Near the ground it is the same as Extra. From 5,000 ft above the ground it downloads the photos at Sharp's detail, and from 25,000 ft at Balanced's. High up, X-Plane still asks for full-detail photos even though you cannot see that detail from there, so Dynamic downloads about half as much in the climb and the cruise. When you descend, it goes back to full detail early, so the ground is sharp again before you get close to it.

## Why it matters on a jet flight

Picture a short airliner hop. You take off, and in about twenty minutes you are at 35,000 ft. You stay up there for most of the flight, with the ground almost seven miles below you, and the descent and approach take the last half hour or so. In a jet, most of the flight is spent high above the ground.

Up there you see whole valleys and coastlines, but not single houses or field edges. X-Plane still asks for the photos at the same full detail it would want at 1,000 ft. Until now OrthoStreamXP did exactly that, so most of what it downloaded in the cruise was detail no one could see. It used your connection and processor and filled the cache, and it could hold up the photos you actually look at.

With Dynamic, the photos follow your height. In the climb and cruise they come at Sharp's and then Balanced's detail, which still looks good from that high, and the downloads drop by about half. Once you start down, Dynamic looks a few minutes ahead and goes back to full detail early, so the approach, the runway and the area around the airport are as sharp as with Extra. You get the same view from your seat, with much less downloading in the middle of the flight.

## Good to know

- Your preset is not changed by the update. To try Dynamic, choose it in **Settings** while OrthoStreamXP is stopped. It applies from the next start.
- Dynamic uses the same cache as Extra, so photos you already downloaded with Extra are still used, and switching between the two downloads nothing again.
- Photos that are already in the cache are shown as they are, at any height. Dynamic only changes what is downloaded new.
- A photo X-Plane loaded at lower detail high up stays that way until X-Plane loads it again. This is why Dynamic goes back to full detail well before the ground comes close on a descent.
